# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-06)

First release.

- Menu bar app for Viggo. The menu bar shows the current or next lesson, and after school the everyday schedule (dinner, quiet hour, lights out).
- Panel with a "Now" card, the rest of the day as one timeline, tomorrow, homework, messages and evening duty. Every section can be switched on or off, with a large text option.
- Log in on Viggo's own page (UNI-Login, SkoleIT or password). No passwords are stored. The session is kept alive, and when it runs out you get one notification and log in again with one click.
- Friends: a friend logs in on their own account in a private window, and you see their day in the same layout, plus when you are both free.
- Apple Calendar import into separate calendars (schedule, homework, events, everyday), kept up to date, and removable again.
- Notifications for moved or cancelled lessons, new homework and new messages, and a morning and evening brief.
- Tick homework as done from the panel.
- Unread messages and questionnaires shown in the menu bar.
- Optional Focus during lessons through two Shortcuts.
- Start at login.
